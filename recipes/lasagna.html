Lasagna

Description

This classic lasagna recipe features layers of rich meat sauce, creamy ricotta, and melted cheese baked to perfection.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  3. Prepare the meat sauce in a pan.
  4. Layer noodles, sauce, and cheese in a baking dish.
  5. Bake for 45 minutes until golden and bubbling.
